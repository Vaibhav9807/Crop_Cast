Forecast interpretation

Forecasts are model estimates and may change. A 7-day outlook is less certain than a near-term forecast. Check official India Meteorological Department (IMD) warnings before spraying, irrigation, harvesting or other high-impact work.

How this works: CropCast uses a transparent heuristic score from forecast temperature, rainfall, broad season fit and water access. It is not a machine-learning prediction and scores are not probabilities of crop success. It does not yet model cultivar, sowing date, soil texture, pests or farm-level microclimate.

Weather data quality

Forecast values are requested from Open-Meteo and shown with the data timestamp. Use the refresh control to retrieve updated data. This website does not independently verify forecast accuracy against observed weather.
